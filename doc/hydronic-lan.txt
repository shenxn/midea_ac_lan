Kitchen AC hydronic module (AC / 22397091 / subtype 1)

The optional water thermostat, auxiliary heat, energy saving, away switches,
and inlet/outlet temperature sensors share the existing kitchen AC LAN socket.
Select hydronic_power and the three hydronic switches in device options, and
select the two hydronic temperature sensors. A separate child device is created.
Only select one entry for a shared physical module.

The adapter polls water telemetry (0x12) and uses existing general/C002 queries
for module presence, water setpoint capability and reported function states.
Controls use individual C002 masks. Setting 25-60 C in whole degrees selects
manual water regulation without enabling heating or changing the AC setpoint.
No cloud account, vendor Lua, optimistic status, or extra socket is introduced.
Water temperature is never used to infer compressor activity.

Validation: independent exact-model command vectors, corrupt/truncated frames,
signed temperature decoding, capability guards and live LAN setpoint readback.
Physical verification kept module heating and auxiliary heat off. Activation of
heating/auxiliary heat has not been tested as part of the migration.

Existing cloud entities must be migrated explicitly; this component does not
silently rename or remove entities belonging to another integration.
